CORVEX delivers technical services that keep critical operations running — safely, precisely, and to the specification that was agreed. Six service lines. Three sectors. One accountable contract.

01Who we are

CORVEX is an engineering and energy services company working across the oil & gas, industrial and infrastructure sectors. Our teams are drawn from operating companies and EPC contractors, which is why we plan around the reality of a running asset rather than the assumption of a greenfield one.

We take on work that carries consequence: turnarounds on live plants, pipeline integrity on operating corridors, structural and civil packages that must stand for decades. Scope is frozen before mobilisation, assumptions are written down, and the handover dossier is assembled as the work proceeds rather than after it.

04Delivery method

Our method is repeatable across every service line, which is what allows a lesson learned in one location to transfer without re-invention.

  • Assess — survey the asset, capture actual condition, and freeze the scope before mobilisation.
  • Engineer — design and plan against the applicable code, with independent verification before issue.
  • Execute — deliver under permit with QA gates at defined milestones, not at completion.
  • Sustain — hand over documented evidence and support the operating phase.

Standards applied — ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 aligned management systems, with ASME B31.3, ASME IX, API 1104, API 570, API 653, API 579-1 / ASME FFS-1 and AWS D1.1 applied as the work requires.

05Quality, safety and documentation

  • Inspection and test plans are written per package and agreed before work starts, with hold points enforced.
  • Welding is controlled at procedure level: every joint traceable to a qualified WPS, a certified welder and a recorded inspection result.
  • Material certification and traceability are retained for every delivered component.
  • Non-conformances are dispositioned with root cause analysis and verified closure.
  • Handover dossiers — ITPs, NDT records, calibration certificates and as-builts — are assembled during the work.
