HSE Policy

Health, Safety and Environment — Corporate Policy Statement

Document reference: CX-HSE-POL-001
Issued: February 2026  ·  Revision 01
Owner: HSE & Quality Directorate
It is CORVEX policy to conduct all operations in a manner that protects the health and safety of our people, our clients' people and the public; that minimises environmental impact; and that complies with applicable law and client requirements. No task is so urgent that it cannot be done safely.

SECTION 01Policy commitment

CORVEX is committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for all persons who may be affected by our activities, including employees, contractors, clients and members of the public. We will conduct operations in a manner that avoids damage to the environment and conserves resources where reasonably practicable.

We recognise that the accountability for health, safety and environment rests with line management at every level. Safety leadership is not delegated below the person who owns the work — supervisors are expected to be visible on the workfront rather than behind a desk.

SECTION 02Health and occupational safety

  • Every task is risk-assessed and permitted before work begins, with the assessment carried to the workface.
  • Every worker holds stop-work authority and may exercise it without consequence or detriment.
  • Competence is verified before mobilisation: certification is matched to the task risk assessment and re-checked at renewal.
  • Personal protective equipment is specified per task, not per site, and its use is supervised.
  • Fitness-for-duty and fatigue management controls are applied on shift work and extended campaigns.

Our safety statement — Safety First. Safety for Me. Safety for All.

SECTION 03Environmental protection

We will identify environmental aspects and impacts for every project before mobilisation and apply the hierarchy of controls — elimination, substitution, engineering control, administrative control — in that order.

  • Waste is segregated, tracked and disposed of only through licensed handlers, with consignment records retained.
  • Spill prevention and response plans are maintained on every site with equipment available at the point of risk.
  • Emissions, noise and dust are controlled on plant and temporary power, particularly adjacent to occupied areas.
  • Reportable environmental events are notified to the relevant authority in accordance with local regulation.

SECTION 04Hazard identification and risk management

hazards are identified through job hazard analysis, task-based risk registers, and shift handover review. Each workfront maintains a live risk register reviewed at every handover and whenever conditions change.

  • Simultaneous-operations planning is mandatory where work affects live plant or occupied areas.
  • Changes of scope trigger a re-assessment — an accepted variation does not carry the old risk assessment.
  • Leading indicators (near misses, observations, stop-work actions) are monitored alongside lagging indicators.

SECTION 05Employee responsibilities

  • Work only within the limits of your competence and the certification you hold.
  • Read and follow the permit, the risk assessment and the method statement for your task.
  • Report hazards, near misses and incidents promptly — same shift, without fear of blame.
  • Exercise stop-work authority where you believe a task is unsafe.
  • Use and maintain issued protective equipment.

SECTION 06Incident reporting and emergency preparedness

All incidents and near misses are reported within the shift in which they occur. Immediate hazards are contained first, then the scene is preserved so far as practicable for investigation.

  • Investigation is proportionate to severity and employs root cause analysis rather than attribution.
  • Corrective actions are assigned an owner and a due date, and are tracked to verified closure.
  • Lessons are distilled into a safety alert issued across all active sites, not confined to the originating project.
  • Emergency response plans, muster arrangements and drills are maintained for every worksite and rehearsed on a defined cycle.

SECTION 07Regulatory compliance

Applicable statutory and regulatory requirements are identified per project before mobilisation and incorporated into the project execution plan alongside client-specific standards. Where standards conflict, the more stringent requirement applies unless the client directs otherwise in writing.

SECTION 08Continuous improvement

  • Independent audits of every worksite on a fixed cycle, with findings graded by risk and closed against evidence.
  • Management review of HSE performance at defined intervals, with actions tracked to closure.
  • Trend analysis of incidents, near misses and audit findings across sites to detect systemic issues early.
  • Revision of this policy at least annually, or following a significant incident or change of scope.
Approved on behalf of the Board — Managing Director  ·  Corporate HSE Directorate
