Quality Policy

Quality assurance and quality control — framework statement

Document reference: CX-QUAL-001
Issued: February 2026  ·  Revision 01
Owner: HSE & Quality Directorate
CORVEX will provide services and deliverables that meet the agreed specification, applicable codes and regulatory requirements, first time and every time — through a quality system that produces evidence as the work proceeds rather than documentation after it.

SECTION 01Our quality framework

Quality and safety share a failure mode: both degrade when documentation is produced after the fact. Our QA/QC approach keeps inspection and test plans attached to the work, so evidence exists at the moment of inspection rather than in a report days later.

  • Quality management system aligned to the principles of ISO 9001, maintained and reviewed on a defined cycle.
  • Documented responsibilities, with a named quality authority per package.
  • Competence requirements defined per role and verified against records.
  • Management review of quality performance and audit findings.

SECTION 02Inspection and test control

  • Inspection and test plans (ITPs) written per package and agreed with the client before work starts.
  • Hold points and witness points enforced — work does not proceed past an unclosed hold point.
  • Non-destructive testing performed to recognised schemes with qualified personnel and calibrated equipment.
  • Calibration certificates retained for all measurement and test equipment.

SECTION 03Material and weld traceability

Every delivered component is traceable to its certification. Welding is controlled at procedure level: joints are recorded against a qualified welding procedure specification, a qualified welder, and an inspection result.

SECTION 04Non-conformance management

  • Non-conformance is reported against the specification, not against an internal expectation.
  • Root cause analysis is performed for recurring and significant non-conformances.
  • Disposition is assigned by a competent authority and verified before the work proceeds.
  • Corrective actions are tracked to verified closure with evidence.

SECTION 05Statement on certification

Where CORVEX holds third-party certification, we report it precisely: the issuing body, the scope of certification and the expiry date. Certification is never implied beyond the scope granted. Quality systems applied to client work are described in the project execution plan for that contract.

Approved on behalf of the Board — Managing Director  ·  Quality Assurance & Compliance Directorate
